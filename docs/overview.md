# Overview tab

## Overview charts

- Activity stacks the calendar above the weekday by hour section, each at the full panel width. The container is `.activity-body`, so the layout follows the panel, not the window.
- The calendar shows one year at a time. A row of year buttons, newest first, lists only the years with timed matches and wraps onto more rows when there are many. The newest year is selected by default. Left and Right arrows move between the buttons and select the one they reach. Any filter change gives a new timed list, so the selection goes back to the newest year.
- The Matches, Hours and Win rate toggle sits on the calendar's heading row and only changes the calendar. Each grid has its own legend as its last row, starting at the first cell column, 6px under the grid. Matches and Hours show five level swatches with a tick between each pair giving the first match count (or the duration, such as "23m" or "1.6h") of the next level, and Win rate shows a RED to BLU ramp plus a faded swatch labeled "<3" for cells under 3 results (its tip says so). The legends have no unit captions, because the headings already name the unit.
- Legend swatches match the cell size, capped at 16px. The calendar's legend uses its own cut points, and each hour grid uses its own.
- The weekday by hour section has two grids, Matches on the left and Win rate on the right, side by side once the panel is 900px wide and stacked below that. Each has its own small heading, legend and edge bars. Hours mode does not apply here. The hour bars sit under each grid and the weekday bars to its right.
- The period buttons on the weekday by hour heading row set the scope of both grids: All time, Year, or Month. Year uses the calendar's selected year. Month adds a select listing the months of that year that have timed matches, newest first, and defaults to the newest. Changing the calendar's year while Year or Month is active moves the scope to that year, and Month resets to its newest month. The grid headings name the scope ("Matches, 2026", "Win rate, Jan 2026"), and the quantile cut points come from the scoped matches. A scope with no matches shows "No matches in this period".
- Matches, Hours and Win rate are the calendar's three modes. Hours shades by time played and labels totals with the duration. Matches and Hours use the 25th, 50th, 75th and 90th percentiles of their non-zero values, so the brightest level holds only the busiest tenth. The calendar's cut points are taken from every year, so the years compare on one scale. The hour grids use the match counts of their cells.
- Win rate mode tints each cell by its rate. Cells with fewer than 3 results are drawn faint. Edge bars always set their length by match count.
- Calendar days after today, and days before the first match in the upload, are empty outlines with no tip. Today has a tan outline. Days with matches are buttons that open the Matches tab searched for that day.
- The line under the calendar summarises the selected year: matches, days played, the busiest day and the longest run of days with matches.
- Form draws matches per month as bars, named by the chart legend. It has no note under the chart.
- Form and Per match have one point per calendar month, from the first to the last timed match. A month with no value leaves a dashed, dimmer gap. A month with fewer than 5 matches that have a result is a hollow point, and its tooltip gives the sample size.
- Matches without a timestamp are left out of Activity, Form and Per match. Activity shows a note with the count.
